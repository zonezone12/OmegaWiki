## My notes

Parrinello is one of the most influential figures in computational chemistry and condensed matter physics. His earlier work on the Parrinello-Rahman barostat (1981) is foundational for NPT simulations. Metadynamics (Laio & Parrinello, 2002) and well-tempered metadynamics (Barducci, Bussi & Parrinello, 2008) are among the most widely used enhanced sampling methods. The OPES framework represents the next generation of this research program.
