## My notes

Invernizzi is the primary developer of OPES, which he introduced first as a metadynamics-like method (J. Phys. Chem. Lett. 2020) and then extended to the unified expanded-ensemble framework in Physical Review X 2020. The OPES implementation in PLUMED is actively maintained and widely adopted.
