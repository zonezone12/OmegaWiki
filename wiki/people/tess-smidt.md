## Recent work

Key developer and maintainer of e3nn (equivariant neural network library in PyTorch). Developed Euclidean Neural Networks and Tensor Field Networks framework. Co-developer of the e3nn library used by NequIP, MACE, and many other equivariant models.

## My notes

A central figure in the equivariant neural network community. The e3nn library that Tess co-developed with Mario Geiger underpins NequIP and much of the equivariant ML force field ecosystem. Her work on Euclidean Neural Networks (Tensor Field Networks) established the theoretical foundations used in NequIP.
