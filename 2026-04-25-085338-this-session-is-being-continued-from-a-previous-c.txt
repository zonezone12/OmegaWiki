  Technical Foundations Developed

  EquivariantBeadNet (EBN) Architecture
  - Input: P beads × N atoms, all Cartesian coordinates + periodic box
  - Layer 1-5: E(3)-equivariant message passing (NequIP-style) on all-atom
  all-bead graph
  - Layer 6a: Force prediction head (standard)
  - Layer 6b: NQE correction head (outputs ΔE, ΔF, σ_F uncertainty)
  - Permutation invariance: bead indices are arbitrary; DeepSets aggregation
  ensures invariance
  - Key insight: bead spread σ_i is a learned signal of prediction uncertainty

  Data Requirements
  - PIMD trajectories: 10,000+ frames per system at 1-2 temperatures, P=32 beads
  - Reference: high-level quantum chemistry (DFT or CCSD(T))
  - Baseline: equivalent classical MD trajectories for comparison

  Validation Metrics
  - Force MAE < 10 meV/Å on test set
  - Energy MAE < 5 meV per atom
  - Bead spread correlation to prediction error: Pearson > 0.7
  - Transfer to new systems: metrics maintained within 20%
  - Computational cost: inference < 2× classical MD
